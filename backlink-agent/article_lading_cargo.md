1. **Predictive Analytics:** Using data to choose sailings that have a higher on-time performance record.
2. **LCL Consolidation:** For small and medium enterprises (SMEs), buyer’s consolidation services are essential to minimize costs without sacrificing speed.
3. **Customs Pre-Clearance:** Ensuring all documentation is handled before the vessel even docks.
